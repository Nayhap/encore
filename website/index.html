0:32 The song map

Every line gets a mark. Repeats stand taller.

Encore lays each synced line on the timeline. Gaps are instrumental. A line sung twice is a hook, three times or more is the chorus. Point at a line to find every place it comes back.

2:40 Details

Built to stay open all day.

Native playback
Audio is decoded in Rust and streamed into a disk cache. The next song loads before this one ends.
System media controls
Media keys, Now Playing on macOS, the media flyout on Windows, MPRIS on Linux.
Lyrics from two places
YouTube Music’s timed lyrics first, then synced lyrics from LRCLIB, matched by title, artist and length.
Your whole library
Playlists, liked songs, albums, artists and subscriptions. Brand channels work too.
Sessions stay with you
Kept in the macOS Keychain, Windows Credential Manager or your Linux keyring. Encore has no servers and sends no analytics.
A queue that behaves
Drag to reorder, play next with ⇧Enter, and shuffle, repeat and autoplay that do what they say.

  • Linux.deb or .AppImage Needs WebKitGTK 4.1, and GNOME Keyring or KWallet to stay signed in.
